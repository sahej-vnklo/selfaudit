Codex Deck 2 · $2M Pre-Seed Narrative

SelfAudit is building the operating intelligence layer for growing companies.

A tighter investor sequence around the real thesis: founders do not need more dashboards. They need a business state engine that tells the truth, diagnoses the cause, and moves them toward the next decision.

01 · Problem
There is no affordable system for founders to know the true state of their business in real time.
The Data Exists
It is trapped in fragments.
Dashboards, reports, CRMs, inboxes, support queues, finance tools, manager updates, and founder instinct all hold pieces of the truth.
The Signal Arrives Late
Numbers appear before meaning does.
Dashboards show numbers. Reports arrive late. Managers give fragments. Nobody connects the operating pattern early enough.
The Cost Compounds
By the time it is explained, it is expensive.
Churn, pipeline risk, support drag, and cash pressure are visible before they hurt. Founders usually see them after they already have.

The problem is not missing data. It is missing operating truth.

02 · Category Definition

The new category is operating intelligence.

A persistent business state engine that learns how the company works, watches what changes, connects what no single tool can see, and tells the founder the truth while there is still time to act.

01

02
Brutal Truth

03
Strategy Intelligence
Built-in business expertise that accelerates decisions before problems become expensive.
Not software around the business. Intelligence inside the business.
03 · Competition

No direct competition. Only incomplete substitutes.

Advisors Diagnose, but do not live inside the data.

High-context humans are expensive, episodic, and slow to ramp.

BI Tools Visualize, but do not reason.

They explain one domain at a time and still require the founder to interpret the pattern.

Dashboards Report what happened, not what to do.

They make the problem visible after someone knows where to look.

SelfAudit Owns the missing layer.

Continuous diagnosis, brutal truth, root-cause reasoning, and strategy execution across the whole business.

This is not a crowded market. It is a category waiting to be owned.
